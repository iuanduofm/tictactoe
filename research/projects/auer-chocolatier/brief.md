# Project brief: Auer Chocolatier — full history

- **Research question and intended reader:** What is the full documented history of Auer Chocolatier (founding, people, locations, products, ownership, closures and changes), from its earliest record to the present? / General reader (assumed; no audience was specified).
- **Deliverable and format:** Dated history dossier in Markdown, each dated statement linked to a source ID.
- **Scope:** All eligible public sources, all languages, founding to present. Exclude unsourced social-media claims and marketing copy as evidence (may be cited as the company's own account).
- **Unit of coverage:** One dated historical event or fact about the company; duplicates merge when the same event is reported by the same original account.
- **Denominator method:** Open-ended scope. Coverage reported as unknown; no percentage claimed.
- **Completion:** Every decisive or conflicting date verified by research-verifier; conflicts kept side by side; coverage stated as unknown with the source families surveyed.
- **Governing package and latest state:** New project.
- **Operating limits:** Public or already-accessible sources; no spending; no external contact; no personal data about private individuals beyond what public records state.
- **Execution:** scouts and coverage analyst on haiku; verifier on sonnet; coordinator on opus; up to 6 workers; ledger at research/projects/auer-chocolatier/ledger/; cadence per session; time zone: owner's (not specified).
- **Stop authority:** The requesting user, by instruction.

## Assumptions
- Reader: general. Language: English output; sources in any language.
- No spending or external contact (defaults).
